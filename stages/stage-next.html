Looking ahead

Where this could go

A living page. It always sits just after the latest finished stage, and holds the directions I'm reading into but haven't built a demo for yet — so it's honestly labelled as "not done," not as another stage.

Where I am

Six stages took me from "the model has no memory" to "the model edits my files and a second model checks its work." The thread running through all of it: a capable model is only half the system — the harness around it is where most of the real engineering lives. Everything below builds on that idea.

Directions I'm eyeing

These are candidates for future stages, not promises. Each becomes a real stage only once there's a runnable demo and at least one honest lesson behind it.

DirectionThe question it answersStatus
Spec-Driven Development How rigorously can I specify intent before an agent acts — instead of steering it turn by turn? Reading
Loop engineering What does it take to run a harness on a schedule, where the system finds its own work and decides when to stop? Reading
Multi-agent systems When is it actually better to split a job across several specialized agents than to make one agent smarter? Curious
Evals & measurement How do I know a prompt or harness change made things better, not just different — with numbers, not vibes? Curious
Local & open models How far can I get running models on my own hardware (Ollama already showed up in Stage 4) before a hosted API is worth it? Curious

How a direction becomes a stage

The bar is the same one every existing stage cleared: a small thing built and broken for real, redacted code in demos/, and a write-up with the gotcha left in. When one of the rows above clears it, it graduates to a numbered stage — and this page moves one step further down the road.
